[caniuse](https://caniuse.com/?search=ext-shader-texture-lod) · [mdn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=EXT_shader_texture_lod WebGL extension) · [spec](https://registry.khronos.org/webgl/extensions/EXT_shader_texture_lod/)

The `EXT_shader_texture_lod` extension for WebGL 1.0 and 2.0 contexts adds texture functions to the OpenGL ES Shading Language such that the shader writer explicitly controls the level of detail (LOD).
