[caniuse](https://caniuse.com/?search=arianotify) · [mdn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=ariaNotify()) · [spec](https://w3c.github.io/aria/#ARIANotifyMixin)

The `ariaNotify()` method of `Element` and `Document` requests assistive technology software, if activated, to announce a message to the user. This can help make dynamic content changes more accessible to users.

- ``api.Document.ariaNotify`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=api.Document.ariaNotify)
- ``api.Element.ariaNotify`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=api.Element.ariaNotify)
