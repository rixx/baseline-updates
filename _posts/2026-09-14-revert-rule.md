[caniuse](https://caniuse.com/?search=revert-rule) · [mdn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=revert-rule) · [spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-cascade-5/#revert-rule-keyword)

The `revert-rule` CSS keyword resets a property to its previous value in the cascade, as if the current style rule had not been present. It's especially useful with conditionals, to cancel a declaration when the condition is not met.
