[caniuse](https://caniuse.com/?search=dir) · [mdn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=dir) · [spec](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/dom.html#the-dir-attribute)

The `dir` global HTML attribute sets an element's text direction to left to right (`ltr`), right to left (`rtl`), or based on the content (`auto`). Elements generally inherit their parent's direction when the attribute is omitted.
