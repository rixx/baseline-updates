[caniuse](https://caniuse.com/?search=intl-locale-info) · [mdn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=Intl.Locale info) · [spec](https://tc39.es/ecma402/#locale-objects)

The getter methods of the `Intl.Locale` API provide supplemental information about a Unicode locale, such as the locale's calendar (for example, the first day in a week or the weekend start day), writing direction, 12- or 24-hour cycles, and numbering system.

### Source features

- ``javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getCalendars`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getCalendars)
- ``javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getCollations`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getCollations)
- ``javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getHourCycles`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getHourCycles)
- ``javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getNumberingSystems`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getNumberingSystems)
- ``javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getTextInfo`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getTextInfo)
- ``javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getTimeZones`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getTimeZones)
- ``javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getWeekInfo`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=javascript.builtins.Intl.Locale.getWeekInfo)
