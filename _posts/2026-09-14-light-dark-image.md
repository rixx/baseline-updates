[caniuse](https://caniuse.com/?search=light-dark-image) · [mdn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=light-dark() image values) · [spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-5/#typedef-light-dark-image)

The `light-dark()` CSS function accepts, in addition to colors, two `<image>` values, such as a gradient or URL, and uses one depending on the current color scheme.

- ``css.types.color.light-dark.image_value`` [[mdn]](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/search?q=css.types.color.light-dark.image_value)
